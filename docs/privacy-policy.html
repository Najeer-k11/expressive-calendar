Privacy Policy — Expressive Calendar

Last updated: 19 November 2026

Expressive Calendar ("the app") is an open-source calendar app for Android, published by Najeer-k11 ("we"). This policy explains what data the app handles and why. The short version: we do not collect, store or share any personal data. The app has no accounts, no servers of its own, no ads and no analytics.

Calendar data

The app shows, creates, edits and deletes events in calendars that are already on your device. It does this through Android's built-in calendar storage (CalendarContract), and only after you grant the Calendar permission (READ_CALENDAR / WRITE_CALENDAR).

  • Your events are read and written on your device only. The app never sends them to us or to anyone else.
  • If a calendar belongs to an account such as Google, Samsung or Exchange, that account's own sync service may sync your changes. That sync is handled by the account provider under its own privacy policy, not by this app.
  • You can revoke the Calendar permission at any time in Android Settings → Apps → Expressive Calendar → Permissions.

Public holidays (internet use)

The app uses the internet for one thing: downloading lists of public holidays for the country you choose. To do this it contacts one of these public services and tells it only which country (and, for Nager.Date, which year) you want holidays for:

  • Nager.Date — https://date.nager.at
  • Google Calendar public holiday feeds — calendar.google.com, used for countries Nager.Date does not cover (Google Privacy Policy)

These requests contain no personal information, no calendar data and no identifiers. As with any internet request, the service you connect to can see your IP address. Holiday lists are cached on your device. You can turn holidays off in Settings, and then the app makes no network requests at all.

Settings stored on your device

Your preferences (theme, colours, first day of the week, chosen country, visible calendars, and so on) and the cached holiday lists are stored privately on your device. If you have turned on Android's device backup, Android may include these settings in your own Google backup. We never have access to them.

What we do not do

  • No analytics, crash reporting or tracking SDKs
  • No advertising or advertising IDs
  • No accounts or sign-in
  • No selling or sharing of data with third parties
  • No access to your location, contacts, files, camera or microphone

Children

The app is a general-purpose productivity tool and is not directed at children under 13. Because it collects no personal data, it collects none from children either.

Deleting your data

The app keeps no data about you anywhere except on your device. To remove everything it has stored, clear the app's storage or uninstall it. Events you created stay in your device or account calendars until you delete them there, just like events created with any other calendar app.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the "Last updated" date above.
